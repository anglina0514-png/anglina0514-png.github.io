# Contact Specification

## Overview
- Target: `#final`.
- Interaction model: static with restrained hover.

## Structure
- Sparse dark closing frame.
- NING wordmark, contact action and copyright occupy separate vertical zones.
- Glass N contracts to a small mark or outline and cannot overlap contact copy.

## Responsive
- Desktop: wide negative space and bottom-aligned utility links.
- Mobile: centered stack with at least 24 px spacing between wordmark, action and copyright.
